U.S. House Election 2022

Equal-area district cartogram, 118th Congress · after Karim Douieb. Geometry built with mapshaper, analysis in base R.
traditional cartogram
Each state is rescaled so its area matches its seat count, then subdivided into equal-area cells — one per district — so a dense urban seat reads as loudly as an empty rural one. Drag the slider to morph, click a state to zoom in, hover a district for the result.
