Guess the Parties

Eight questions about who actually belongs to each party. Almost everyone gets them wrong in the same direction, so the interesting number is not whether you miss — it is by how much, and on which groups. Answer all eight before you reveal anything.

Out of every 100 people who identify with the party, how many belong to the group? Type a whole number from 0 to 100. There is no scale and no list of options to choose from — that is how the question was asked in the original study, and how it was asked of this class.

The questions are from Douglas J. Ahler and Gaurav Sood, "The Parties in Our Heads: Misperceptions about Party Composition and Their Consequences," Journal of Politics 80:3 (2018). True shares are the authors' benchmarks from the 2012 American National Election Study and Pew's 2012 Religion & Public Life Project. The national comparison is their YouGov sample of 1,000 American adults, fielded March 2015.
