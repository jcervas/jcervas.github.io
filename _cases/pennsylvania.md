I served as the [redistricting consultant](https://www.cmu.edu/ips/news-and-events/jonathan-cervas-pa-reapportionment-commission.html)
for the [Pennsylvania Legislative Reapportionment Commission](https://www.redistricting.state.pa.us/).
The LRC developed state Senate and House of Representatives lines for the 2020 decade.

Those maps were [approved via a bipartisan vote](https://www.redistricting.state.pa.us/commission/article/1096),
and the Pennsylvania Supreme Court
[unanimously affirmed](https://www.pacourts.us/Storage/media/pdfs/20220316/195610-lrcpetitonsforrevieworder.pdf)
their constitutionality.
