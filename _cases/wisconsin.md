I was [appointed court co-consultant to the Wisconsin Supreme Court](https://acefiling.wicourts.gov/document/eFiled/2023AP001399/745286). I was
tasked with evaluating and reporting to the court on remedial proposals submitted by parties to the
case.

The litigation involved a remedy for both the state Assembly and Senate maps after the Court found
the prior maps consisted of non-contiguous districts in violation of the state constitution. New
plans were adopted for the 2024 elections.
