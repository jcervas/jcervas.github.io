I was [an expert witness](https://iapps.courts.state.ny.us/nyscef/DocumentList?docketId=rPiOl6sOYhi1LxUiSDWpBA==&display=all&courtType=Nassau%20County%20Supreme%20Court&resultsPageNum=1)
for the plaintiffs in this challenge under the New York Voting Rights Act.
[Read more about the case](https://www.npr.org/2024/02/07/1227586316/nassau-county-state-voting-rights-act-new-york)
with coverage from NPR.

The case was settled, and the challenged map was replaced with one whose features mirrored those of
my illustrative map.
