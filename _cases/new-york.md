I was [appointed Special Master](https://iapps.courts.state.ny.us/nyscef/ViewDocument?docIndex=ffzj4348UcVesF4ziHLc1g==)
by the Supreme Court of New York, tasked with drawing the state's 2022–2030 Congressional and State
Senate maps after the legislature's plans were struck down. The maps were implemented for the 2022
election cycle.

For a detailed archive of press coverage of this work, see the
[New York 2022 media collection]({{ '/media/ny-2022/' | relative_url }}).
