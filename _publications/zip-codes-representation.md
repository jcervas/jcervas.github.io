---
title: ZIP Codes as Geographic Bases of Representation
authors: Bernard Grofman and Jonathan R. Cervas
venue: Election Law Journal
year: 2021
kind: article
order: 10
link: https://jonathancervas.com/2020/zipcodes/ZIP.pdf
---

Weighs the desirability of using ZIP codes as the geographic basis for congressional districts and finds them a poor foundation: voters do not know their boundaries, they cut across cities and counties, and they do not align with census geography.
