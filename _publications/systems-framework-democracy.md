---
title: A Systems Framework for Remedying Dysfunction in U.S. Democracy
authors: Samuel S.-H. Wang, Jonathan R. Cervas, Bernard Grofman, and Keena Lipsitz
venue: Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences 118, no. 50
year: 2021
kind: article
date: 2021-12-01
link: https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2102154118
---

Outlines a systems-level modeling approach to understanding and repairing failures of democratic representation — elite polarization, unresponsive representatives, and the ability of a faction to gain power at the expense of the majority — treating electoral institutions as interacting parts whose reforms must be evaluated together rather than in isolation.
