Using a probit model of candidate choice applied to 2020 election data, the paper estimates the second choices of third-party voters and finds that Libertarian Jo Jorgensen's presence likely cost Donald Trump victory in Arizona and Georgia — an argument that Republicans, and Trump in particular, would benefit from ranked-choice voting.
