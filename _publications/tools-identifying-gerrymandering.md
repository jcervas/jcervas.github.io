  Districting in Pennsylvania

venue: Political Geography 76
year: 2020
kind: article
date: 2020-03-01
link: https://linkinghub.elsevier.com/retrieve/pii/S0962629818303342
---

Uses Pennsylvania's congressional maps from League of Women Voters v. Commonwealth of Pennsylvania (2018) as a case study to develop and apply tools for detecting partisan gerrymandering, comparing the 2011 map, the proposed party remedies, and the court-drawn 2018 plan.
