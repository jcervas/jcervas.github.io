Examines the implications of the Pennsylvania Supreme Court's decision in League of Women Voters v. Commonwealth of Pennsylvania (2018) — which struck down the state's congressional map as an unconstitutional partisan gerrymander on state-law grounds — for future partisan-gerrymandering litigation in other states with similar constitutional provisions.
