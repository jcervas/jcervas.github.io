title: Are Presidential Inversions Inevitable? Comparing Eight Counterfactual Rules
  for Electing the U.S. President

year: 2019
kind: article
date: 2019-06-01

Asks whether presidential inversions — when the popular-vote winner loses the Electoral College — are inevitable, comparing eight counterfactual rules for electing the president to see how often each produces an inversion.
