---
title: The Unanticipated Effect of COVID-19 on House Apportionments
authors: Jonathan R. Cervas and Bernard Grofman
venue: Social Science Quarterly 102, no. 5
year: 2021
kind: article
date: 2021-09-01
link: https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=3898284
---

Shows how small, pandemic-driven shifts in measured population can flip the rounding-sensitive allocation of U.S. House seats after a decennial census, highlighting an unanticipated effect of COVID-19 on apportionment.
