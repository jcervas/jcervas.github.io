Every state keeps a list of its registered voters, but who may have it, how you ask for it, and what it costs differ from state to state. Each link below goes to the state’s own election office page, form, or statute.

As of November 16, 2026. Rules change often; check the state’s page before relying on any row.
“Cap” marks a per-record charge with a printed maximum that a statewide file always reaches, so the amount shown is that maximum.
